# Job Search – Daily Shortlist

A weekday-updated shortlist of quality, compliance, vigilance, complaint management, QMS, CAPA, labeling and post-market surveillance roles in medical devices and pharma for the Greater Chicago area (Illinois or US-remote, $100K+ listed pay or not listed).

**Live report:** https://niransh84.github.io/Job_Search/job_search_R.html

## What's in the report

- Shortlist of roles at about 70%+ fit
- Notable roles under 70%
- Dropped roles, each with a reason
- Sorted by fit
- Sources: LinkedIn, employer career sites, Indeed, and BioSpace

## How fit is scored

| Factor | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Required skills | 30 |
| Overall requirements | 25 |
| Experience | 20 |
| Industry | 10 |
| Title / seniority | 8 |
| Preferred skills | 7 |

Location and salary are gates. Posting age is shown separately and is not part of the fit score.

## Update cadence

The report is refreshed weekday mornings around 8 AM Central. `job_search_R.html` is overwritten in place, so the live link stays the same.

## Privacy

No personal contact information is included.
